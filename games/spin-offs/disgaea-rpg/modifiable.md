# Disgaea RPG — What Can Be Modified

**Out of scope here.** Disgaea RPG is an online, server-backed game. Changing its client, files or network traffic
breaks its terms of service and affects other players, so this wiki doesn't document it.

What these pages do cover: reading its data and art offline (from your own install) for reference, and using that art in
**other** games' mods, which is how Disgaea 5 and Disgaea D2 mods use it. See [data.md](data.md) and [assets.md](assets.md).
