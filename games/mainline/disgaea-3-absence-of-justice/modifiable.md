# Disgaea 3: Absence of Justice — What Can Be Modified

No public mods or tools for Disgaea 3 were found (2026-11-04). Changing files needs a modded PS3 or Vita, or an
emulator (RPCS3 for PS3, Vita3K for Vita). Several archives are stored encrypted on the Vita.

The RPCS3 forums have a [compatibility thread](https://forums.rpcs3.net/showthread.php?pid=279545) for the PS3 release.
