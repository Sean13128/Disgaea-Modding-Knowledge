# Classic Disgaea 5 Enhanced (CD5E)

- **Author:** iHaku (with helpers credited in the post) · **Game:** Disgaea 5 Complete, Steam · **Version described:** 1.2 (May 2019, "probably final till 2.0")
- **Get it:** the author's [Steam discussion post](https://steamcommunity.com/app/803600/discussions/0/1648792158828778141/) (Google Drive link) · **License:** none stated

This page documents what the mod does and how it interacts with the game. It does not reproduce the mod.

## What it does
- Aims for the feel of the classic games while keeping D5's quality-of-life features: more grinding, fewer exploits.
- Removes or limits mechanics that let veterans skip post-game content.
- Much stronger Item World enemies.
- Rebalanced evilities; more prominent status ailments.
- More distinct generic classes (though most still share the same three unique evilities).
- Removes Overloads.
- Adds Tyrant Valvatorez, Pure Flonne and others from the Special Content shop as permanent playable units (with some skill restrictions), and makes Pricurry playable.

## How it loads
Distributed as files to replace; v1.1 came with an "Important Info read first + Changelog" document warning about a
softlock with the new characters. Which files it replaces isn't stated in the post.

## What it touches
Evility, class, enemy and item data (by its feature list). The author mentions a spreadsheet of skill damage, vanilla
evility and unit data, and says individual files could be extracted for others.

## Compatibility
Likely to overwrite other mods' changes to the same game files. Made before D5 Mod Kit existed.

## Lessons for modders
Shows how far data edits alone (2019, before the Kit's exe patches) could rebalance D5, including turning
Special-Content characters into permanent units.
